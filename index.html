01 / WHO WE ARE

Not just another
server.

BRTX

is a community made for gamers who want somewhere that actually feels alive.

Whether you're locking in for ranked, chasing a clean Rocket League play, or dropping into Fortnite with friends, BRTX is built around one simple idea: gaming is better with people.

No corporate esports act. No forced seriousness. Just a clean space to meet people, play games, share clips, talk nonsense and build a community.

08 / QUESTIONS

You've got
questions.

What is BRTX?+

BRTX is a gaming community centered around people, conversation and games including Valorant, Rocket League and Fortnite.

Is BRTX an esports organization?+

No. BRTX is a community. The focus is on hanging out, finding people to play with and building a fun gaming space.

How do I join?+

Hit any Discord button on this site. It takes you straight to the BRTX community.

Do I need to play one of the featured games?+

No. The featured games are part of the community's rotation, but the point is meeting people and having a good time.
