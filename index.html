FAQ

Questions?

What is BRTX?

BRTX is a gaming community focused on bringing people together to play games, chat and hang out.

What games does BRTX support?

BRTX currently features VALORANT, Rocket League and Fortnite. The community can also talk about and play other games.

Is BRTX an esports organization?

No. BRTX is a gaming community, not an esports organization.

How can I join?

Simply use the Discord button on this website to join the BRTX community.

Who runs BRTX?

Batrix is the founder, 9ue5 is the developer, and fr3on is the head admin.
